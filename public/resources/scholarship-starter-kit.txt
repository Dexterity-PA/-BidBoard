MERITOUSLY: A STUDENT'S SCHOLARSHIP SEARCH CHECKLIST
Free resources: https://meritously.com/for-counselors

1. Write down your graduation year, enrollment plans and relevant interests.
   Keep sensitive information private. You do not need to share financial
   records or account passwords to use this checklist.

2. Find a few possible awards: https://meritously.com/scholarships/directory
   Start with college awards and outside programs connected to your circumstances.

3. Open each provider's official website. Confirm the current application
   cycle, eligibility, deadline, time zone and application method.
   If a rule is unclear, record a question for the provider.

4. List what you must submit. Check for a separate application, school
   nomination, essay, transcript, recommendation or project.

5. Choose one next action and a target date. Request documents early.
   Tracker: https://meritously.com/resources/scholarship-tracker.csv

6. Write honestly. Answer the actual prompt and follow the award's rules
   about assistance. Proofread before submitting.
   Essay guide: https://meritously.com/blog/how-to-write-a-scholarship-essay

7. Save your submitted application and confirmation. Check receipt of
   supporting materials and read any follow-up instructions.

8. Compare confirmed offers against the full estimated cost of attendance.
   Keep loans, work earnings and unconfirmed awards separate from grants.
   Worksheet: https://meritously.com/resources/award-comparison.csv
   Walkthrough: https://meritously.com/blog/compare-scholarship-offers

9. Read renewal and acceptance requirements before relying on future funding.
   Renewal guide: https://meritously.com/blog/scholarship-renewal-requirements

Meritously is a research and planning resource. The award provider's current
rules control eligibility and selection. Some catalog dates are unconfirmed
or from an earlier cycle. Meritously does not guarantee awards.
