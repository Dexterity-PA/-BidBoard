MERITOUSLY — A 20-MINUTE SCHOLARSHIP PLANNING SESSION
Fall 2026 / students planning 2027 college entry
Companion shortlist: https://meritously.com/for-counselors#seasonal-planner
Official sources last checked October 29, 2026. Recheck before sharing or applying.

GOAL
Leave with one appropriate application and a concrete next action. The shortlist is a selection of five different application routes, not a ranking or an exhaustive list. Meritously does not administer the awards or guarantee eligibility or selection.

MINUTES 0–4: CHOOSE A PROFILE
Use a fictional student if working in a group. Do not collect or share citizenship documents, household income, student logins or other personal records.
Example A: A spring-2027 senior with strong grades who needs substantial aid.
Example B: A senior choosing between college admission-based aid and an outside leadership competition.

MINUTES 4–9: CHECK THE OFFICIAL SOURCE
Choose an entry from the companion shortlist. Open the provider's source.
- Is this the correct graduation and enrollment cycle?
- Which eligibility rule might exclude the student?
- Is financial need required, considered, or not used for this award?
- Is the date a scholarship deadline or a college admission priority date?
- What time and time zone apply? Write "unconfirmed" if the provider does not say.

MINUTES 9–14: WORK BACKWARD
Write the deadline, an earlier personal submission target and the next action.
Does the student need portal access, a teacher recommendation, a counselor report or official test scores? Identify who controls each missing item. The student's own submission may not complete the file.

MINUTES 14–18: CHECK COST AND EFFORT
Is the award annual or total? Does it cover tuition or broader costs? What would remain to pay? Are there renewal, enrollment, interview or service requirements? Open the full program rules rather than guessing from the scholarship name.

MINUTES 18–20: CHOOSE AND RECORD
Record one action with a target date in the downloadable planner. The CSV is a private working copy after download; it will not refresh when provider terms change. Keep any filled-in personal copy out of public sharing.

BEFORE DISTRIBUTION
Recheck official dates. Dates marked passed belong to a prior application window and must not be promoted as upcoming. Share the unfilled worksheet or public page; do not publish students' completed trackers. No account is needed to use the worksheet.
